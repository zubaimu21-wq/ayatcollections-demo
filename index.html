Jewellery Care Tips

Keep It Dry

Take your jewellery off before washing hands, bathing or swimming. Water and soap dull the gold-tone finish over time.

Perfume First, Jewellery Last

Apply perfume, lotion and hairspray before putting on your pieces, and let them dry. Chemicals are the main cause of tarnish.

Store Each Piece Separately

Keep every piece in its pouch or box so stones do not scratch and chains do not tangle. Wipe with a soft dry cloth after wearing.
